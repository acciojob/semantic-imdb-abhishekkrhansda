Summary

Interstellar follows Cooper, a former NASA pilot and farmer, who joins a group of astronauts on a journey through a mysterious wormhole near Saturn. Their mission is to find a new habitable planet for humanity as Earth becomes increasingly difficult to survive on.

Actors

Matthew McConaughey plays Joseph Cooper, a former NASA pilot who leads the mission.

Anne Hathaway plays Dr. Amelia Brand, a scientist and astronaut who joins Cooper on the mission.

Jessica Chastain plays Murphy Cooper, Cooper's daughter who grows into a brilliant scientist.

Michael Caine plays Professor John Brand, a NASA scientist working to save humanity.

Favorite Quote

"Love is the one thing that we're capable of perceiving that transcends dimensions of time and space."

This quote highlights one of the movie's central themes: the powerful connection between people despite distance and time.
